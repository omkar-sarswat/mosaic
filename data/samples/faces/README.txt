Synthetic faces of people who do not exist (StyleGAN). No real person's face is used.

- synthetic_face_a.png  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:GAN_Mensch_StyleGAN2.png (public domain)
- synthetic_face_b.jpg  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:This_Person_Does_Not_Exist_example.jpg (public domain)
- gallery/gan_*.jpg     generated by https://thispersondoesnotexist.com (StyleGAN2), resized to 512 px.
                        Two images that looked like minors were discarded; no child's face is used as a suspect.
- probes/*.jpg          made by drishti/vision/gallery.py: the faces above shrunk, tilted, blurred, noised,
                        darkened and JPEG-compressed into a 1280x720 frame with a camera caption, to imitate
                        an ATM/branch CCTV still and a photo found on a seized phone. gan_10 appears in a probe
                        but is enrolled on no record, to show a face that returns no candidate.

The demo build enrols these as photographs on record of the synthetic suspects in the five
agency cases (see DEMO_PHOTOS in drishti/vision/gallery.py). Used only to demonstrate and test
face detection and matching.

- portraits/p_*.jpg     the portrait pool: more faces from https://thispersondoesnotexist.com (StyleGAN2),
                        fetched by scripts/fetch_portraits.py and resized to 512 px. manifest*.json records each
                        file's apparent gender and age band. Those labels come from the ONNX Model Zoo
                        age_googlenet / gender_googlenet models (data/models). They are used only here, offline,
                        to sort these synthetic images; DRISHTI never runs them on evidence or on anyone's photo.
                        Faces the model reads as under 25 are dropped when fetched. review.json then lists the
                        faces a person left out on the contact sheets (scripts/review_portraits.py): any that
                        still looked under 21, headwear or sunglasses, turned or posed faces, and uncertain labels.
                        `drishti portraits` (or the demo build) gives one portrait to each person already in an
                        investigation's record (accused in a case record, on a case, or ranked in a case's
                        network), matched by gender and apparent age, with a source and legal basis like any
                        photograph on record. Nobody outside an investigation gets one. The dossier labels each
                        as a synthetic portrait.
